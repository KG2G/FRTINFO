RELEASED

AR-15 platform

- Super Safety
    • Created by Hoffman Tactical
    • Requires low shelf lower
    • Requires trigger cut
    • Requires full auto/M16 profile BCG
    • Geissele trigger compatible (with a trigger cut and hammer blocker)
    • 3 position crossbolt, push button safety
    - Atrius Super Selector
    • Requires low shelf lower
    • Requires full auto/M16 profile BCG
    • 3 position traditional safety selector
    • Geissele trigger version is available (includes a hammer blocker)
    - Cornfire MARC
    • Atrius clone
    • Lever made from S7 tool steel
    • Requires low shelf lower
    • Requires full auto/M16 profile BCG
    • 3 position traditional safety selector
    - SplitFire RS
    • Compatible with semi auto, but full auto/M16 profile BCGs is recommended
    • Lower reciever shelf doesn't matter
    • 3 position traditional safety selector
    - KIKASS
    • Requires full auto/M16 profile BCG
    • Lower reciever shelf doesn't matter
    • 3 position traditional safety selector
    - ASDesigns ARC Fire
    • Requires low shelf lower
    • Requires trigger cut, includes a cut trigger
    • Requires full auto/M16 profile BCG
    • Geissele trigger compatible (with a trigger cut and hammer blocker)
    • 3 position traditional safety selector
    • V2 allows for 45 degree lever throw
    - Arizona Regulator
    • Drop in
    • Lower reciever shelf doesn't matter
    • Requires full auto/M16 profile BCG
    • 3 position traditional safety selector
    - Kabuto/TURD/ARSS15
    • Drop in
    • Most have 3D printed parts, but there are all metal versions from other vendors
    • 3 position traditional safety selector
    • Does not work with M16 pocket lowers
    • Requires full auto/M16 profile BCG
    • Geissele trigger compatible (over travel device/blocker required)
    • Can be printed on your own, different variants exist for printing including, high shelf, larue and geissele support.
    - Cassette style triggers
    (have milspec selector options from Datum Machine Works)
    • Rarebreed FRT-15/Delta Team Tactical - FRT-15L3
    • Triggered Company (Partisan Triggers) - The Disruptor
      (Has milspec pin kit and selector directly as available accessories as well)
    • Sylvan Arms - SADRT (Two position only)
    - Trigger Kicker
    • Created by Hoffman Tactical
    • Works with milspec triggers
    • Requires full auto/M16 profile BCG
    • Replaces the disconnector and disconnector spring
    • Currently two position with three position is in development
    - Many .223/5.56 set ups would need a H2/3 buffer to prevent bolt bounce
    - AR9 builds should refer to Blowback9 for more information.
    - AR22 builds should refer to Borebuddy for more information.

    HK MP5 and clones platform

    • 3D printed separate lower that accepts AR-15 FCG and a super safety/ARC called the Leber V2, can print it yourself; a trip for all models. Fullsize models also need a buffer. Available for purchase as well.
    • Metal lower from ASDesigns; accepts AR-15 FCG and a super safety/ARC and requires a trip for all models. Fullsize models also need a buffer. Made by the creator of the Leber V2, s3igu2.
    • Lee Sporting lower with super safety; requires a trip for all models. Fullsize models also need a buffer. This route isn’t recommended by the creator of the MP5 FRT, s3igu2. Atrius support is now available.
    • Rarebreed has a lower that uses a standard MP5 housing.
    • Pembleton & sons released a drop in super safe compatible cassette for the OEM MP5 lower. Using a modified trigger and milspec ar15 hammer. 3 position upgrade is later.
    • ASDesigns is also working on a drop in cassette variant called the ARHK that works in standard MP5 and other similar HK lowers.
    • Parabellum Combat Systems is also working on a drop in variant that works in standard MP5 lowers.
