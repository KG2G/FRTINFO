RELEASED

AR-15 556/9mm/22lr pretty much every caliber

- Tons of places for purchase and many different variants.

- Super Safety
  • Requires low shelf lower
  • Requires Trigger Cut
  • 3 position crossbolt, push button safety
- Atrius Super Selector
  • Requires low shelf lower
  • 3 position traditional safety selector
- Cornfire MARC
  • Atrius clone
  • Lever made from S7 tool steel
  • Requires low shelf lower
  • 3 position traditional safety selector
- ASDesigns ARC Fire
  • Requires low shelf lower
  • Requires Trigger Cut
  • 3 position traditional safety selector
  • V2 allows for 45-degree lever throw
- Arizona Regulator
  • Drop-in
  • Lower Shelf doesn't matter
  • 3 position traditional safety selector
  • 3 position version is available
- Kabuto/TURD/ARSS15
  • Drop-in
  • Most have 3D printed parts, but the ARSS15 by bssquirts is all metal
  • 3 position traditional safety selector
- Cassette style triggers
  • Rare Breed FRT-15
  • Delta Team Tactical FRT-15L3
  • Dairyland Defense FRT-15
  • Partisan Triggers The Disruptor
- AR9 and AR22 setups require more specific details, please research that

AR-10

  • Grey Market Research has Super Safety sets for AR-10. 🔗 Footage

AK

  • Tons of places for purchase
  • Normally 2 position, but 3 position is possible and requires a decent amount of work and separate FA parts including the selector and disconnector 🔗 Footage

HK MP5 and clones platform

  • 3D printed separate lower that accepts AR-15 FCG called the Leber V2, tons of places for purchase.
  • Metal lower from ASDesigns, made by the creator of the Leber V2. 🔗 Footage
  • Lee Sporting lower with Super Safety, can be bought complete by War Hammer Armaments or suppressortools.com. Note that this route isn’t recommended by the creator of the MP5 FRT, s3igu2.
  • Rare Breed has a lower in development that uses a standard MP5 housing. 🔗 Footage
  • ASDesigns is also working on a drop-in variant called the ARHK that works in standard MP5 and other similar HK lowers. 🔗 Footage by @s3igu2 on x.com
  • Parabellum Combat Systems is also working on a drop-in variant that works in standard MP5 lowers. 🔗 Footage

Steyr AUG

  • A few places for purchase, hole drilled into stock is required. 🔗 Footage

Springfield Kuna

  • Requires a separate lower and trip from Nexus Firearms, this allows for either Rare Breed or Super Safety function and CZ EVO Magpul PMAGs capability 🔗 Footage
  • ASDesigns is also working on a lower.
