RELEASED

AR-15 556/9mm/22lr pretty much every caliber

- Tons of places for purchase and many different variants.

- Super Safety
  • Requires low shelf lower
  • Requires trigger cut
  • 3 position crossbolt, push button safety
- Atrius Super Selector
  • Requires low shelf lower
  • 3 position traditional safety selector
- Cornfire MARC
  • Atrius clone
  • Lever made from S7 tool steel
  • Requires low shelf lower
  • 3 position traditional safety selector
- ASDesigns ARC Fire
  • Requires low shelf lower
  • Requires trigger cut
  • 3 position traditional safety selector
  • V2 allows for 45 degree lever throw
- Arizona Regulator
  • Drop in
  • Lower shelf doesn't matter
  • 3 position traditional safety selector
  • 3 position version is available
- Kabuto/TURD/ARSS15
  • Drop in
  • Most have 3D printed parts, but there are all metal versions from other vendors.
  • 3 position traditional safety selector
- Cassette style triggers
  • Rarebreed FRT-15
  • Delta Team Tactical FRT-15L3
  • Dairyland Defense FRT-15
  • Partisan Triggers The Disruptor
- AR9 and AR22 set ups requires more specific details, please research that

AR-10

  • Grey Market Research has super safety sets for AR-10.
  • In development by Partisan Triggers
  • In development by Atrius
  • ASDesigns is working on a lever for ARC Fire support

IMG 12 Gauge AR-10 Upper

  • Works with the Grey Market Research has super safety sets for AR-10, may work with other solutions in the future.

AK

  • Tons of places for purchase
  • Normally 2 position, but 3 position is possible but requires a decent amount of work and separate FA parts including the selector and disconnector
  • In development by Partisan Triggers

HK MP5 and clones platform

  • 3D printed separate lower that accepts AR-15 FCG called the Leber V2, tons of places for purchase.
  • Metal lower from ASDesigns, made by the creator of the Leber V2.
  • Lee Sporting lower with super safety, can be bought complete by War Hammer Armaments or suppressortools.com. Note that this route isn’t recommended by the creator of the MP5 FRT, s3igu2. Atrius support is now available.
  • Rarebreed has a lower that uses a standard MP5 housing.
  • ASDesigns is also working on a drop in variant called the ARHK that works in standard MP5 and other similar HK lowers.
  • Parabellum Combat Systems is also working on a drop in variant that works in standard MP5 lowers.

Steyr AUG

  • A few of places for purchase, hole drilled into stock is required to allow for super safety compatability.
  • Red Star Palmetto is offering a progressive trigger option for pre-order.

Springfield Kuna

  • Requires a separate lower and trip from Nexus Firearms, this allows for either Rarebreed or super safety function and CZ EVO Magpul Pmags capability
  • ASDesigns is also working on a lower.

SIG MCX platform

  • There is a trip bar you can install in the upper which allows usage of a Rarebreed.
  • ASDesigns has a separate trip in the lower that allows for super safeties and their ARC selector.
  • In development by Partisan Triggers

Taurus TX22

  • Tons of places for purchase.
  • Don't get it from FFT, it's very expensive and considered the "gen 1" variant of the trip
  • The new versions of the trip is much easier on the internals
  • There are weighed striker assemblies which allows for suppressor and subsonic ammo use
