RELEASED


AR-15 556/9mm/22lr pretty much every caliber

- Tons of places for purchase and many different variants. - Super Safety - Requires low shelf lower - Requires Trigger Cut - 3 position crossbolt, push button safety - Atrius Super Selector - Requires low shelf lower - 3 position traditional safety selector - Cornfire MARC - Atrius clone - Lever made from S7 tool steel - Requires low shelf lower - 3 position traditional safety selector - ASDesigns ARC Fire - Requires low shelf lower - Requires Trigger Cut - 3 position traditional safety selector - V2 allows for 45 degree lever throw - Arizona Regulator - Drop in - Lower Shelf doesn't matter - 3 position traditional safety selector - 2 position verion is avaliable - Kabuto/TURD/ARSS15 - Drop in - Most have 3D printed parts, but the ARSS15 by bssquirts is all metal - 3 position traditional safety selector - Rarebreed FRT-15 - Delta Team Tactical FRT-15L3 - Dairyland Defense FRT-15 - Partisan Triggers The Disruptor - Cassette style trigger - AR9 and AR22 set ups requires more specific details, please research that
